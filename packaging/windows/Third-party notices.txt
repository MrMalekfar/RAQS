RAQS Research Workbench ships the Qt and PySide6 libraries.
The libraries are dynamically linked and remain separate files under
runtime\Lib\site-packages\PySide6 and runtime\Lib\site-packages\shiboken6.
The package license texts are in the corresponding .dist-info\licenses
directories. You may replace those libraries with compatible modified builds
and reverse engineer RAQS to debug such modifications as permitted by the LGPL.

PySide6 and Shiboken source (6.10.3):
https://download.qt.io/official_releases/QtForPython/pyside6/PySide6-6.10.3-src/

Qt source (6.10.3):
https://download.qt.io/official_releases/qt/6.10/6.10.3/submodules/

CPython is distributed with its license in runtime\LICENSE.txt. Version and
license information for the vendored Python packages is located in their
respective .dist-info directories.
